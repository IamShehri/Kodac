# Kodac Name Clearance Gate — 2026-08-11

## Decision

```text
RED — KODAC IS NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLIC BRAND INVESTMENT
```

This is a preliminary project-governance assessment, not legal advice and not a substitute for a trademark attorney's clearance opinion.

## Evidence reviewed

- USPTO guidance states that likelihood of confusion can arise from marks that are similar in sound, appearance, meaning, or commercial impression, and recommends comprehensive clearance searching.
- USPTO TTABVUE records show Eastman Kodak Company requested an extension of time to oppose U.S. application serial 87342545 for the word mark `KODAC` in 2017.
- The same USPTO record now reports that application/registration as `CANCELLED - SECTION 8`; cancellation does not erase the historical evidence that Kodak treated `KODAC` as relevant enough to consider opposition.
- USPTO TTABVUE shows more than 100 proceedings involving Eastman Kodak Company and includes recent activity against marks such as `KODIAK` in 2026 and historical activity involving `KODACLOUD`, `KODATOUCH`, and other KODA/KODAK-adjacent marks.
- Kodak's official brand FAQ states that Kodak trademarks and logos require permission/licensing.

## Governance consequence

Until professional clearance is obtained:

- `Kodac` may remain an internal/working repository name and engineering codename.
- Do not make irreversible public-brand investments such as paid campaigns, trademark filings, large merchandise orders, or expensive visual identity work under this name.
- Do not imply affiliation with, endorsement by, or permission from Kodak.
- A founder decision to continue technical development under the working name does not equal legal clearance.

## Closure condition

G1 closes only after a qualified trademark professional performs a relevant-jurisdiction clearance search for the intended software/AI/developer-tool goods and services and records a usable conclusion.
