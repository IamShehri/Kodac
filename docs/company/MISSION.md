# Kernux Mission

Kernux helps engineering teams build reliable, measurable, cost-aware AI products.

## Mission Statement

Give every AI product team the operational control plane they need to ship AI features with confidence.

## Who Kernux Serves

Kernux serves teams that own production AI systems:

- Startup founders shipping AI-native products.
- Product engineers adding LLM workflows to existing applications.
- Platform engineers standardizing AI usage across teams.
- Engineering leaders who need reliability, quality, cost, and governance visibility.

## What Kernux Must Do

Kernux must make AI systems:

- Observable: every request can be traced.
- Measurable: quality, cost, latency, and usage can be tracked.
- Comparable: prompts, models, and providers can be evaluated against each other.
- Controllable: routing, fallback, caching, and rate limits can be governed.
- Auditable: teams can explain what happened and why.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Why | Benefits | Risks | Alternatives | Trade-offs | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anchor the mission on engineering confidence | The buyer cares about production safety, not generic AI enthusiasm | Clearer sales narrative and product prioritization | May sound less exciting to non-technical buyers | Productivity-focused mission | Confidence is durable but requires proof through reliability | 1 day |
| Make cost, latency, and quality first-class | AI operations fail when these are scattered across provider dashboards and app logs | Strong dashboard and alerting foundation | Quality metrics are harder than cost/latency | Start with cost only | Quality work takes longer but differentiates the platform | 1-2 weeks across MVP |
| Treat governance as a future expansion, not the MVP core | Compliance buyers need depth that a one-month MVP cannot support | Keeps scope realistic | May delay enterprise conversations | Build governance first | Simpler MVP, less enterprise polish early | Revisit after beta |

## Mission Guardrails

- Kernux is not a model provider.
- Kernux is not a full APM replacement.
- Kernux is not a no-code AI app builder.
- Kernux is not a research experiment tracker first.
- Kernux is the operational layer around AI applications.
